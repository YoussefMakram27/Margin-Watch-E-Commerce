# Project Brief: Margin Watch: E-Commerce

## Business Context
A mid-size online retailer operating across multiple countries (US, UK, Germany, Egypt,
India, Canada, Australia) sells products across 8 categories — Electronics, Clothing,
Home & Kitchen, Beauty & Personal Care, Sports & Outdoors, Toys & Games, Books &
Stationery, and Groceries. The company has ~1,800 customers and processes thousands of
order lines across 2023-2025, with sales split across Consumer, Corporate, and Home
Office segments. Leadership has pulled a raw export from the order management system to
understand overall performance — revenue, profitability, returns, and customer behavior
— but the data has never been cleaned or analyzed, and it isn't yet clear which
categories, regions, or channels are actually driving (or hurting) the bottom line.

## Objective
Analyze the raw order data to identify where the business is profitable, where margin
is eroding, and what's driving the difference.

## Key Questions
- Which categories and regions generate the most revenue, and which generate the most profit?
- Is discounting eating into margin more in some categories than others?
- What is the return/cancellation rate, and which segments or products are driving it?
- How does order value and profitability differ across customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)?
- Are there seasonal or monthly trends worth flagging?

## Audience
Portfolio reviewers (recruiters/hiring managers) — written to demonstrate a full,
structured analysis workflow from raw data to business recommendations.

## Definition of Done
A cleaned dataset, a documented cleaning log, a KPI dashboard, and a findings report
with 3-5 numbers-backed insights and recommendations.
