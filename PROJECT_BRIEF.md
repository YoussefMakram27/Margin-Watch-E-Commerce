Business Context:

A mid-size online retailer operating across multiple countries (US, UK, Germany, Egypt,
India, Canada, Australia) sells products across 8 categories — Electronics, Clothing,
Home & Kitchen, Beauty & Personal Care, Sports & Outdoors, Toys & Games, Books & Stationery, and Groceries.
The company has ~1,800 customers and processes thousands of order lines across 2023-2025,
with sales split across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
Leadership has pulled a raw export from the order management system to understand overall performance — revenue,
profitability, returns, and customer behavior — but the data has never been cleaned or analyzed,
and it isn't yet clear which categories, regions, or channels are actually driving (or hurting) the bottom line.
